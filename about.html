About 关于

An independent luthier.

Peng Lei builds traditional American-style steel-string guitars by hand, in small numbers.

独立制琴师。

彭磊以手工、小批量制作传统美式钢弦吉他。

One person builds the guitar

He works alone. The person you write to is the person who makes the instrument. The name on this site is a maker, not a factory brand.

一把琴由一个人做完

他一个人做。你写信的对象，就是做琴的人。本站这个名字是制琴师，不是工厂品牌。

The American steel-string idiom

The outlines follow that tradition: dreadnought, OM, 000, slope-shoulder. Wood, bracing, and scale are decided for the commission in front of him, not copied from a public price sheet.

美式钢弦的做法

外形走这一传统：Dreadnought、OM、000、斜肩。木材、支撑和有效弦长，按眼前这张委托来定，不从公开价目表上抄。

English is the full text

Each page carries English and Chinese together. The English is complete on its own, so a player abroad can read the site without a translator. Chinese says the same things beside it. Write in either language.

英文是完整正文

每一页都同时有英文和中文。英文可以单独读完，人在国外不必先找翻译。中文在旁边说同样的事。用哪一种语言写信都可以。

No dealer list

There is no wall of shops or agents. The way in is the inquire page, or the email address on every page.
