Instruments 作品

Traditional steel-string forms, built one at a time.

Peng Lei builds dreadnought, OM, 000, and slope-shoulder guitars. Body, scale, and wood are chosen for the piece you commission.

These are traditional body names, not model numbers, and this page is not a price list.

传统钢弦形制，一次一把。

彭磊制作 Dreadnought、OM、000 与斜肩吉他。箱体、有效弦长和木材，按你委托的那一把来定。

这些是传统箱体名称，不是型号。本页也不是价目表。

Dreadnought D 型

Wide shoulders and a deep lower bout. The large American steel-string voice: a low end that can carry flatpicking or sit with a singing voice.

肩宽，下腹深。大型美式钢弦常见的性格：低频能撑住拨片，也能托住人声。

Orchestra Model OM 型

A smaller 14-fret body with a clearer balance across the strings. Often the first shape fingerstyle players ask about.

较小的 14 品接合箱体，各弦之间更清楚。指弹的人常常先问到这一形。

000 小箱体

Close in size to an OM. A 12-fret neck is possible when you want the bridge in a warmer position and a more intimate balance.

尺寸与 OM 接近。若希望琴桥位置更暖、声音更贴身，可以做 12 品接合。

Slope-shoulder 斜肩

The shoulders fall away from the neck instead of standing square. An older, softer outline, built when that shape is the one you want.

肩从琴颈处斜下去，不是方肩。轮廓更早、更柔。你要的就是这个形制时才做。

Woods, bracing, scale length, nut width, and trim belong in the letter. They are agreed for one guitar, not chosen from a menu here.

木材、支撑、有效弦长、琴枕宽度和配饰写在信里谈。它们为一把琴商定，不在这里做菜单。
